UV FIX - mirrored text (updated)
================================

Fix #1 (previous)
-----------------
Banner and GroundDecal are double-sided: every quad is duplicated with
reversed winding, and both copies referenced the SAME UV indices, so the
back-facing copy showed the texture mirrored. The back-facing duplicates
were given horizontally mirrored UVs (u -> 1-u). That fixed the Banner.

Fix #2 (this update) - GroundDecal
----------------------------------
The ground strip was still mirrored on BOTH sides, so fix #1 only made the
two faces consistently wrong. The cause was the V axis, not U: the decal
lies in the XZ plane, and its UVs ran with v increasing toward +Z. For an
up-facing quad that gives a UV frame with the opposite handedness to the
face normal, i.e. a reflected texture. Depending on where you orbit the
camera it reads as a left-right flip or an upside-down flip - both are the
same reflection seen from different sides.

Both GroundDecal quads now use v -> 1-v (v increases toward -Z), with
dedicated vt entries instead of reusing indices from the poles:

  f 274/282 275/283 276/284 277/285   <- down-facing (-Y)
  f 277/281 276/280 275/279 274/278   <- up-facing (+Y)

Verification: for every face, cross(dP/du, dP/dv) . N > 0 now holds on the
Banner and on both GroundDecal quads. The text on the ground now reads in
the same direction as the text on the banner, i.e. correctly for a viewer
standing on the +Z side of the gate.

The PNG textures were never the problem; they are correct as authored.
Only the .obj files changed.

Reading direction
-----------------
If you want the ground text to read from the OTHER side of the gate (-Z),
swap the two u values instead: use u -> 1-u on both quads as well. That
rotates the decal 180 degrees without reintroducing the mirror.

Poles
-----
Some pole faces also have mirrored UVs, but they sample a thin featureless
strip at the bottom of the texture (v = 0.00-0.05), so there is nothing
readable to flip. Left untouched.

If it still looks mirrored
--------------------------
Some importers convert right-handed OBJ to a left-handed engine space by
negating the X axis, which mirrors everything. The gate is now internally
consistent, so if BOTH the banner and the ground read backwards after
import, flip the BaseColor PNG horizontally once and both will be correct.
